orders
(
    order_id,
    customer_name,
    product_name,
    category,
    amount,
    quantity,
    city,
    payment_method,
    order_status,
    order_date
)

Questions

1. [3 Marks] – Multiple Filter Logic

Find orders where:

category is Electronics or Furniture
amount is greater than 25000
quantity is not 1
order status is Delivered

Display:

order_id, customer_name, product_name, amount, quantity

2. [3 Marks] – Pattern + Numeric Filter

Find customers whose name contains an (case-insensitive) and whose order amount is between 10000 and 50000.

Display:

customer_name, product_name, amount, city

3. [3 Marks] – Complex OR / AND

Find orders satisfying either of these conditions:

Electronics orders with amount greater than 60000
Furniture orders with quantity greater than 3

But exclude cancelled orders.

Display:

order_id, product_name, category, amount, quantity, order_status

4. [3 Marks] – Date + Multiple Conditions

Find orders placed during 2025, where:

payment method is UPI or Credit Card
amount is greater than 15000
city is not Chennai

Display:

order_id, customer_name, amount, payment_method, order_date

5. [3 Marks] 

Find orders where:

customer name does not start with A
category is not Grocery
amount is either below 10000 or above 50000
order status is either Delivered or Shipped

Display:

order_id, customer_name, category, amount, order_status
